# Projekt Hard — presentation equipment/item bonus changes

Source: https://projekt-hard.eu/presentation (English presentation; accessed 2026-09-04).

## Scope and transcription notes

This report transcribes the presentation's **Equipment Changes**, **High-Level Equipment**, **Changes to weapons above level 25**, and **Spirit Stones** sections. Item rows below preserve the presentation tooltip wording, including `+9`/stone enhancement and required level. The page's tooltip class suffix is visibly truncated for some images (`Warrio`, etc.); where the class is unambiguous from the item, it is normalized to Warrior/Ninja/Sura/Shaman.

## 1. Equipment Changes

The presentation says:

> Changes have been made to many equipment pieces and jewelry to give them new life and better adapt them to Project Hard. Some bonuses were changed and equip level requirements for certain items were standardized.
>
> This was done to prevent a single type of item from dominating, make more item types useful, and allow players to better tailor their equipment to the statistics required by their character classes.

### Earrings

Verbatim rules/change list:

- The equip level for earrings with stats: Wooden / Gold / Copper / Silver has been standardized to level 12.
- The equip level for earrings with stats: Jade / Ebonite / Pearl / White Gold has been standardized to level 33.
- The bonuses of level 33 earrings have been changed - each of these earrings will now grant a character stat and additional HP, as previously seen with Ebonite Earrings.
- The equip level for earrings with stats: Crystal / Amethyst / Blue Tears has been standardized to level 54.
- Strength was added to Amethyst Earrings instead of Vitality, resulting in three earring types for classes:
  - Amethyst - Strength
  - Blue Tears - Intelligence
  - Crystal - Dexterity
- All level 54 earrings received bonuses similar to Blue Tears and Amethyst Earrings, as well as HP from level 33 earrings, to make them more attractive compared to level 33 earrings.

## 3. Changes to weapons above level 25 — PvM values

Verbatim presentation text:

> All weapons above level 25 have been changed to include two additional bonuses: Attack Value PvM and Magic Attack Value PvM.
> This was done so that they can be used as alternatives to level 30 weapons while progressing towards level 75 weapons, which are best for PvM.The changes do not eliminate the ability to use level 30 weapons with high average damage - they will still perform very well if someone acquires such a weapon.
>
> The additional bonus scales with the levels of subsequent weapons, also taking into account their base attack values.
> You can view it on our server Wikipedia in the weapon list or by holding the SHIFT key while hovering over the weapon.
> Weapons do not have any hidden Average Damage or Skill Damage.
>
> Additionally, the attack speeds of individual weapons have been standardized. This means high attack speed weapons won`t dominate anymore, and you`ll be able to see the full variety of available weapons on the server.
>
> In short, this does not mean significantly higher damage than before - the values were adjusted so there is no forced need to use level 30 weapons with Average Damage.
>
> This is our own solution and works great because it separates the PvM aspect from the character`s PvP damage, which is maintained in a very similar format you know from the old days.

**Exact values exposed in this presentation section:** no numeric per-weapon PvM table is printed on the slide. The presentation explicitly directs players to the Server Wikipedia weapon list or to SHIFT-hover the weapon for each exact `Attack Value PvM` / `Magic Attack Value PvM` value. The documented rule is therefore: all weapons above level 25 gain both PvM bonuses; values scale by subsequent weapon level and base attack value; no hidden Average Damage or Skill Damage; attack speeds standardized.
